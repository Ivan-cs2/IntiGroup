About us

We Dig Projects

Founded in Perth in 2017, we’ve found rapid growth through providing solid project delivery to our clients, combining experienced personnel with modern systems and a flexible approach. Our team brings expertise, reliability, and local knowledge to every project, from roadworks, earthmoving, drainage and utilities. We understand the unique demands of working in Western Australia’s diverse environments, and we take pride in meeting deadlines and budgets without compromising safety or quality.

Our strength lies in strong partnerships with contractors, local councils, and developers. We strive to be more than just a subcontractor—we become a trusted extension of your team, working collaboratively to ensure seamless project delivery.

Our commitment to safety, sustainability, and innovation sets us apart. We invest in modern equipment and adopt industry best practices to deliver efficient, environmentally responsible outcomes. Above all, our people are at the heart of what we do—skilled, hardworking professionals who share a passion for building better communities.
