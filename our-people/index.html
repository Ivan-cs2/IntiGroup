Our People

At HCMS, our strength lies in our people. We are a tight-knit team of skilled professionals who share a passion for delivering high-quality civil construction projects across Western Australia. Our collective expertise, dedication to excellence, and commitment to safety are what set us apart.
Experienced Leadership
Our leadership team brings a wealth of experience in civil construction, providing hands-on guidance to ensure every project is delivered on time, on budget, and beyond expectations. Their vision drives our company forward, fostering innovation and excellence in everything we do.

Skilled Workforce
From project managers and engineers to machine operators and labourers, each member of our team plays a vital role in our success. With a strong focus on training, professional development, and workplace safety, we ensure our people are empowered to do their best work every day

Community-Focused
We’re more than a construction company—we’re part of the community. Our team is committed to creating opportunities for local workers, engaging with stakeholders, and contributing to the sustainable growth of Western Australia. We take pride in supporting a number of local community sports clubs in the Perth and wider region.
